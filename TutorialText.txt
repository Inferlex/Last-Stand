Welcome to The Last Stand. This game is about surviving more and more difficult waves of enemies using your strategic thinking.
With this tutorial, you will learn step by step how to play and make you accustomed to the controls.
As you may have noticed, you start with a Base and a hero, who is in this case, a Commander.
Lose all your Bases and you lose the game, so watch out!

Each unit is quite different. The drones are fast and cheap, but fragile. Their power lies in their ability to repair buildings
Initiate are a bit slower than drones, but will take a more beating and have bigger damage.
Templars are slow and dont have a ranged attack, but they are heavily armored and their attacks ignore defense.
Learn their powers and used them to your advantage.

Finally, lets talk about combat.
Enemies can only be seen when they are close to you, so watch out for that.
Your units will automatically attack enemies when they are in vicinity.
Or you can just right click enemies when you have units selected.
+Look! an enemy is attacking you! Kill it!
Excellent!
Every hero has three unique abilites. Your hero, Commander, has Battle cry, Stun grenade and Aura of victory
Battle cry will increase nearby friendly units damage for limited time. Whenever buffed unit kills the enemy, it counts as if hero killed it, so use it frequently.
Stun grenade will slow down and decreases damage of enemy units in the area for a time.
Your ultimate abilty, Aura of Victory, makes your units around you invincible for a time!
